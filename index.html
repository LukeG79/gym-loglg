Programme rules
  • No squats, deadlifts, RDLs, good mornings, hip thrusts or heavy bent-over rows.
  • Stop an exercise if it brings on your familiar radiating pain, tingling, numbness or weakness. Don't train through worsening symptoms.
  • Leg press: moderate load and controlled depth. Stop before your lower back or pelvis position starts to go.
  • RIR means reps in reserve: 2 RIR is about two more good reps in the tank.
  • When you reach the top of the rep range on every working set at the target RIR, add a little load next time.
  • Warm up with 2–5 minutes general work, then 1–3 progressive sets before the first big lift for a muscle.
  • Week 12 is a deload: about half the sets, 3–4 RIR.
